Welcome to Marti Catering & Lounge

Located in the heart of Hawassa, Marti Catering and Lounge brings you the ultimate blend of rich cultural heritage and modern dining comfort. Famous for our authentic traditional platters, exceptional local spices, and warm hospitality.

Whether you are looking to host a grand family gathering through our professional catering services or enjoy a relaxing evening at our vibrant lounge, Marti delivers perfection on every plate.
